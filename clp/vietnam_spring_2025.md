# Certified LeSS Practitioner Course in Vietnam Spring 2025

Many people have expressed interest in my delivering an in-person Certified LeSS Practitioner in Vietnam. This includes people in Ho Chi Minh City, as well as in Hanoi. I have spent several weeks working through the logistical details to make this possible. 

The only significant barrier at this point is ensuring I have enough people for the course(s) to be viable. Most of the interest is coming from a small handful of companies, which are currently working through their budgeting process for 2025. 

I have created this page so that my internal advocates will have a link they can share with their peers and management as they work to gather additional interest, and as they work to ensure sufficient budget is allocated to make the event possible.

I don't currently plan to list the course on the LeSS Company website until I have a bit more clarity on the exact dates and venue.

## Where

If I can find at least 15 people who can attend the course in Ho Chi Minh City, I should be able to run the course. 

If there are just as many people in Hanoi, I should also be able to travel to Hanoi the following week and deliver a second course there.  If there are only a small number of people in Hanoi, it will be more cost effective for them to travel to Ho Chi Minh City instead.

The specific event venue in each city is likely to be a large ballroom at one of several large event hotels. I already have quotes from many of these, with more quotes on the way.

I have contemplated running a course somewhere in the middle of Vietnam easily accessible via train and air from both Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi. A beach resort hotel in Danang would one such option. I strongly suspect key sponsors will prefer minimizing travel for most people by staying in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi instead. Personally, I am willing to do whatever works best for people.

## When

I am tentatively planning on scheduling the in-person 3-day course sometime during the last two weeks of February 2025, although this could change.

Timing considerations include:

* Internal advocates need time to work through their internal budgeting and approval processes.

* I need at least 6 weeks to finalize logistics once enough people have signed up. Most of this is due to the 20 to 30 business days required to obtain the necessary "educational event with foreign element" permit.

* Ideally scheduling the training during favorable weather. I am told the more pleasant dry-season in Ho Chi Minh City is from December to April. This seems to also align with reasonable weather in Hanoi.

* Any minor date adjustments arising from event space availability.


## Pricing

With enough people, I should be able to keep the price to USD$2299/person. 

After currency conversion you will find USD$2299/person to on the lower end of public CLP course prices globally, including those elsewhere within Asia. 

Raising the price as high as USD$2699/person would be a great help in lowering the minimum student thresholds, and still be within a standard global price range. Unfortunately, I believe this would run the risk of being too high for most people within Vietnam to easily gain approval to attend.

Lowering the price below USD$2299/person will make it very difficult for a public course to overcome the high fixed costs of travel and hosting.

## Language

The course will likely be presented entirely in English. 

If one of the sponsors is willing to send a large enough number of participants, and willing to help out  with the translation efforts, it is possible to create a bi-lingual version of the course. I have already done this for Spanish. Repeating the effort for Vietnamese is straightforward as long as I have a Vietnamese native speaker and fellow technologist available to help me with the translation of the written materials.

See the "English and Local Language" section below for more details.

# English and Local Language

The majority of the LeSS related content is in English, including the [LeSS website](https://less.works){:target="_blank"}, [LeSS case studies](https://less.works/case-studies){:target="_blank"}, and [LeSS books](https://less.works/resources/learning-resources/books){:target="_blank"}. My native language is English. I also [know some Spanish](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/learning-spanish-baselang-james-carpenter/){:target="_blank"}.

A small portion of the LeSS content has been translated into other languages. For example, people have graciously translated [my LeSS case study](https://less.works/case-studies/large-server-hardware-company){:target="_blank"} into [Japanese](https://less.works/jp/case-studies/large-server-hardware-company){:target="_blank"} and [Chinese](https://less.works/zh-CN/case-studies/large-server-hardware-company){:target="_blank"}.

That said, I think we can collectively muddle our way through using a mix of English and the local language. My ideas to help us are below.

## Participants Can Help

I'm expecting at least some of the participants to be extremely fluent in English, with everyone else having at least workable English skills. Participants with stronger English skills should be able to translate more confusing aspects in the moment.

## Live Transcription and Live Translation

As long as I bring a little bit of audio gear with me, I am expecting to be able to use Microsoft's [Conversation App](https://translator.microsoft.com/){:target="_blank"} which provides both live transcription and live translation. Microsoft PowerPoint 365 on Mac also supports both live transcription and live translation using the same underlying translation service.

Related Links:
* [Video: Live Conversation App in Use](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MgsSoZZVe2U&t=1s){:target="_blank"}
* [Video: Engage your audience with Live Presentations in PowerPoint](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lzfqwn05Lzg){:target="_blank"}

## Breakout Groups Can Use Local Language
A huge percentage of the course leverages breakout groups for both table discussions and system modeling. Each table should feel free to conduct their table discussions and system modeling activities in whatever language they are most comfortable with.

Class level discussions with me will need to be mostly in English.

## Pre-Reading Content

This course has a large amount of pre-reading content which I will distribute to those who sign up. I expect it is easier for most people with English as a second language to read English, than to hear it. 

Online text translators have become extremely good lately, so that should help with any tricky aspects of the pre-reading. I expect the entirety of the pre-reading content can easily be machine translated.

Being diligent about the pre-reading will go a very long way in helping to follow along during class.

With a bit of help, I may even be able to fully translate all the course materials into the local language.

## Awareness and Sensitivity

By leveraging all the mitigation techniques above, and remaining sensitive to the language issue, I expect we will be able to help each other overcome any language related obstacles we encounter.

Someone who doesn't know any English probably should not attend. That said, I doubt there will be many people who are interested in LeSS who don't know at least some English. If you managed to read everything here so far, your English is probably strong enough to be workable for the course.
