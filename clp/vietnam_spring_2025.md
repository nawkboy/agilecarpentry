# Certified LeSS Practitioner Course in Vietnam Spring 2025

Many people have expressed interest in my delivering an in-person Certified LeSS Practitioner in Vietnam. This includes people in Ho Chi Minh City, as well as in Hanoi. I have spent several weeks working through the logistical details to make this possible. 

The only significant barrier at this point is ensuring I have enough people for the course(s) to be viable. Most of the interest is coming from a small handful of companies, which are currently working through their budgeting process for 2025. 

I have created this page so that my internal advocates will have a link they can share with their peers and management as they work to gather additional interest, and as they work to ensure sufficient budget is allocated to make the event possible.

I don't currently plan to list the course on the LeSS Company website until I have a bit more clarity on the exact dates and venue.

# When

The **last two weeks of February 2025**, although this could change based on the scheduling constraints.

### Primary Scheduling Constraints

* Internal advocates need time to work through their internal budgeting and approval processes.

* I need at least 6 weeks to finalize logistics once enough people have signed up. Most of this is due to the 20 to 30 business days required to obtain the necessary _educational event with foreign elements_ permit.

### Secondary Scheduling Constraints

* I would prefer to schedule the training during favorable weather. I am told the more pleasant dry-season in Ho Chi Minh City is from December to April. This seems to also align with reasonable weather in Hanoi.

* Any minor date adjustments arising from event space availability.


# Where

* Ho Chi Minh City
   * Event hotel in District 1 or District 3

* Hanoi
   * Event hotel in Ba Dính, Hoán Kiem, or Tây Hó.

The abilty to run the course in both Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi assumes there will be enough people in attendance to justify doing so. With smaller numbers it will make the most sense to run a single event instead, presumably in Ho Chi Minh City.

I have contemplated running a course somewhere in the middle of Vietnam easily accessible via train and air from both Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi. A beach resort hotel in Danang would one such option. Personally, I am willing to do whatever works best for people.

I have researched and obtained event space quotes from event hotels in all of the above locations.

# Pricing

With enough people, I should be able to keep the price to USD$2299/person. 

After currency conversion you will find USD$2299/person to be on the lower end of public CLP course prices globally, including those elsewhere within Asia.
