# Training Venue Details

The course is being hosted in a hospitality suite at the [The Statler Dallas](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/dfwstqq-the-statler-dallas/){:target="_blank"} in Downtown Dallas. 

If demand justifies doing so, it is possible I will relocate the course and raise the number of available tickets accordingly. In such an event, I will do my best to minimize any inconvience for those who are traveling to attend. If you are traveling, I suggest you stick with hotel rooms with free early cancelation.

Downtown Dallas is easily accessed from DFW airport [via light rail](https://www.dart.org/guide/transit-and-use/rail/rail-line-details/dart-orange-line){:target="_blank"}.

If you are driving into downtown, you may wish to use a parking application such as [Spot Hero](https://spothero.com/){:target="_blank"} to find a better deal than hotel parking.

## Full Attendance Required

The course runs from 8:30 am - 6:00 pm all three days. **For certification, full attendance is required.** Please take this into consideration when making travel plans. 

Most attendees who are traveling will need to stay Thursday night, unless they take a very late flight home. On the upside, taking a flight home on Friday morning will be super easy.

## Meals

I will be providing lunch for everyone, most likely at nearby table service restaurants within walking distance. I still need to do a bit more research to understand which table service restaurants are within walking distance.

Evening dinners are not included in the cost of the course. During class, I suspect we will end up organizing dinner for those of us who are traveling.
