# Training Venue Details

The course is being hosted in a hospitality suite at the [Hilton Anaheim](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/snaahhh-hilton-anaheim/){:target="_blank"}.

The Hilton Anaheim is [Disneyland Good Neighbor Hotel](https://www.hilton.com/en/hotels/snaahhh-hilton-anaheim/disney/){:target="_blank"}, within an easy walk or shuttle to Disneyland.

John Wayne Airport (SNA) and Los Angeles Airport (LAX) are both reasonable options for anyone who is arriving by plane. [Disney's airport directions](https://disneyland.disney.go.com/guest-services/getting-here/by-plane/){:target="_blank"} are nearly identical to those for the hotel. The Hilton Anaheim has a [shuttle service to/from Disneyland as well as John Wayne Airport](https://myhmrbus.com/){:target="_blank"}.
